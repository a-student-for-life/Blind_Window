    Where it breaks: Godavari, July 2024

    A Sentinel-1 image with a valid pixel at the point arrived about 30 minutes after the peak (2024-07-27 00:31 UTC), but no loaded ESA plan file listed a pass then. Planned coverage pointed to 3 August, so the cloud-aware rule was about 168 hours too pessimistic. Planned passes are therefore a lower bound on what satellites will actually collect.

    Limits

    • Validated on one event (Guwahati). Godavari is a failure case, Ganges is provisional (footprint only, no pixel check).
    • Ensemble numbers are a member spread, not calibrated probabilities.
    • Plan files are the latest published; whether they were available as of the issue date is not verified. Beyond a plan file's end, passes are projected from the repeat cycle and marked as projected.
    • Cloud is a point forecast from Open-Meteo (16 days max). Scene cloud in the replay is whole-scene, not cloud over the river. The 30% cutoff is a fixed rule.
    • "Usable" means a planned pass with a chance of a valid view; it does not mean flooding will be detected.
    • GloFAS suits large rivers. Flash and urban floods are out of scope. Discharge is modelled, not measured.
    • Population and hospitals are nearby context, not people or facilities affected.
    • Landsat is not included in the live outlook (its public calendar gives dates, not pass times).

    Related work

    Forecast-driven satellite planning is not new: the EFAS pre-tasking workflow was developed from 2016 and integrated into EFAS operations in April 2022; a 2013 paper used hydrological forecasts to prioritise acquisitions; NRSC's PLANNeR portal supports acquisition requests in India. Blind Window is a transparent, per-reach view of public-pass gaps that sits alongside these, and its brief is a draft for a planner to review, never a submitted request.

    Data: GloFAS via Open-Meteo Flood API and CEMS EWDS; ESA Sentinel acquisition plans; Open-Meteo / ECMWF cloud forecasts; Copernicus GFM; GHS-POP; OpenStreetMap contributors. GloFAS is informational, not an official warning.
