# Blind event check: rules (draft)

Written before the review starts, as part of ANALYSIS_PLAN.md section 10. Adapted from WAKE
`stage_a/REVIEW_RULES.md` and `review_page/SECOND_RATER_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

## What each rater sees

One card per event: river name (if GloFAS/HydroRIVERS gives one), nearest town, district and
country, the peak date, and the GloFAS hydrograph for 30 days either side with the 2-, 5- and
10-year thresholds drawn. Nothing about satellites, plans, clouds or blind windows.

## Question

"Is there a report of river flooding within 50 km of this place within 7 days of this date?"

- **Yes:** a DFO archive record, a ReliefWeb report, or a news story names flooding of this river,
  or of places within 50 km on it or its floodplain, within 7 days of the date.
- **No:** you searched the sources below and found reports for the region and period that do not
  mention flooding here, or no reports at all for a region that is normally well covered.
- **Can't tell:** sources are too vague to place (for example "floods in northern Bangladesh"
  with no districts) or you could not search.

## Sources, in this order

1. Dartmouth Flood Observatory active archive (search by country and dates).
2. ReliefWeb, country page, disaster and situation reports for the month.
3. A web search: `<river> flood <month> <year>` and `<town> flood <month> <year>`, first two pages,
   in English and one local language if you read it.

Write the source link in the notes for every Yes.

## Working alone

Each rater works alone and does not discuss events until both have finished. Answers are saved by
the review page (Y / N / U keys, notes, CSV download), as in WAKE. Disagreements are looked at
together afterwards; the first answers are what kappa is computed on.

## Time

About 5 minutes per event, 100 events, so 8 to 10 hours per rater. It can be split into
sessions; the page saves as you go.
